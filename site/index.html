The hardest products are the ones nobody wants to design. Regulated. Legacy-bound. Full of edge cases, where an edge case is a satellite or a fine-art shipment crossing a border.

I have spent ten years there. Seven as a designer, the last four running a specialty risk placement platform in the London market: around £200M of premium a year, a thousand brokers, and no patience whatsoever for bad software.

The work is rarely the screen. It was the sixty parcels I posted across the UK to find out which courier actually delivers. The config tool I prototyped because configuration, not design, was the thing capping our growth. The dashboard I argued us out of building.

Find the constraint limiting the system, not the screen in front of you.

Kill the features that do not earn their complexity. Stay close enough to the craft to be useful in the Figma file and the sprint board on the same day.

Then ship it, and go and measure whether it worked.
